# Customer setup rehearsal

Use only staging: https://triumphant-hq-web-preview.triumphantsam30.workers.dev/platform

This is an owner rehearsal first. It does not count as independent customer
acceptance. No purchase, WhatsApp connection, customer message or staff invitation
is part of this session. Use invented business information and no customer data.

## Your scenario

You run **Demo Studio Rehearsal**, a fictional consultation business. Your team
offers free 30-minute consultations, weekdays 10am–4pm Lagos time, with a
15-minute buffer and 24-hour notice. A quote requires the service, location and
preferred date. Prices are confirmed by the team after reviewing the request.

## Tasks

Use the workspace's own labels and guidance. If you cannot work out a task after
three minutes, record where you stopped; do not struggle indefinitely.

1. Sign in using your existing account. Create a separate business named
   **Demo Studio Rehearsal**; keep existing test businesses unchanged.
2. Prepare it for a website assistant and appointments, without colleagues or
   WhatsApp. Save the description and two accurate answers from the scenario.
3. Set the consultation availability, duration, buffer and notice above. Check
   that the saved values match what you intended.
4. Find where to practise answers. Ask one question your business can answer and
   one it cannot. Explain what a visitor should do after an unknown answer.
5. Find the website installation instructions and the launch checks. Explain
   what remains before a real customer can use it. Do not enable an origin,
   publish the page, paste code into a real site or connect a number in this pass.
6. Find enquiries, appointment records, usage limits and support. Explain where
   you would look if a customer says their enquiry is missing.
7. Review Plans without starting checkout. Explain the trial's limits and what
   happens when it ends. If your account is ineligible, record that message;
   do not create another account to evade the one-trial-per-owner rule.

## Report back

For each task, record **done / needed help / blocked**, approximate minutes and
the label or message that confused you. Include what you expected to happen.
Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or screenshots containing private records.

Finish by answering: Could you explain this product to another business owner?
Which step would make you leave? What did you expect it to do that it did not?

Leave the rehearsal business unpublished. Keep the records for review; cleanup
will pause/disable test channels rather than erase evidence without approval.
