## 1. Proposed subscription terms — business owners

Provider: Triumphant Technological Services (TriumphantTech).
Contact: admin@triumphantech.com. Effective date to be assigned at publication.

The service provides approved answers, enquiries, appointment rules, autoresponders,
team handover and internal follow-ups. Each channel requires eligibility, connection
and delivery checks. Setup completion is not a WhatsApp connection. No sales or
revenue guarantee, provider-approval guarantee or uptime SLA is offered.

Customers must be authorised to act for their business, keep information accurate,
invite authorised colleagues and lawfully process customer information. Do not use
the service for spam, fraud, unlawful surveillance or regulated/high-impact decisions.
Do not upload passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.
Payment screenshots are not evidence of settlement.

Proposed monthly prices are NGN 15,000 / 30,000 / 50,000. Show applicable taxes and
the final total before consent. Reply/FAQ limits are 500/20, 2,000/60 and 5,000/150.
Monthly enquiry limits are 100/500/1,500; booking records 100/300/1,000. Ten members
including owner; one eligible WhatsApp number. Meta/provider charges are separate.
Managed service from NGN 75,000 and assisted setup require a separate written scope.

A reply is a metered automated response, not a conversation or guaranteed delivery.
Fallbacks and accepted send attempts can count. Idempotent retries do not count twice.
No automatic overage billing or rollover. Closed enquiries and cancelled bookings do
not replenish counters. Paid counters reset by Lagos calendar month, independently
of the provider billing date. Optional AI matching uses approved facts, with monthly
attempts limited to the plan reply allowance and a shared 500-attempt daily cap.

One explicitly started trial per verified owner/workspace lasts 14 days or 50 replies,
whichever comes first, with 20 FAQs and ten enquiry/booking records each. No card,
automatic charge or paid conversion. Trial counters never reset monthly. Expiry stops
automation and preserves records; trial credits are not Meta messaging credits.

Recurring billing requires explicit consent. Cancel renewal through Plans when
available; verified paid access remains until the recorded end. Existing agreement
amounts remain unchanged. Plan changes require support and an agreed price/date.
Do not start a second agreement to resolve delayed payment verification.

Proposed guarantee: request a full first platform-payment refund within seven days.
Investigate duplicate/unauthorised collections and materially defective or undelivered
service for appropriate remedies. Later ordinary cancellation does not automatically
refund unused time, subject to applicable rights. Provider costs and separately scoped
work are assessed separately. No blanket digital-service no-refund rule applies.
Refunds are operator-assisted; cancellation is not a refund. Provider/bank completion
may take 3–10 working days after initiation. Never request card details or OTPs.

Support hours, initial-response targets and optional help allowances are defined in
the commercial offer, subject to confirmed staffing. They are not repair-time or
24/7 guarantees. Defects, security reports and statutory complaints are not refused
because configuration-help minutes are exhausted. Mandatory rights remain unaffected.
Liability provisions, termination notice and dispute jurisdiction require legal review.

## 2. Platform privacy notice — owners, staff and end customers

Proposed role allocation, to confirm in a processing agreement: the customer business
controls its customer-service purposes; TriumphantTech processes that content to
provide the service and controls its own security, account and billing administration.

Stored categories include verified account/session records; business profiles, FAQs
and settings; memberships, invitations and access history; enquiry contacts/needs;
booking contacts/times/status; rehearsal and connected WhatsApp messages; assignments,
notes/follow-ups; usage; payment references/status and provider identifiers. Card
numbers are not stored. Connection tokens are protected backend credentials.

Use data to authenticate, configure workflows, answer questions, store enquiries and
appointments, enable authorised teamwork, enforce usage and administer payments and
security. Sign-in is not marketing consent. Confirm appropriate lawful bases and
end-customer notices; do not assume consent is the basis for every processing purpose.

| Recipient | Platform purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Website/Worker hosting, D1, protected backups, security checks; optional Workers AI receives questions and approved FAQ context |
| Resend | Sign-in codes and enabled transactional/invitation emails, with necessary recipient/message content |
| Paystack | Payment processing and verified subscription information |
| Meta | Eligible account/number connection and WhatsApp messages when enabled |
| Authorised colleagues | Their business's permitted inbox/conversation/internal work; owner-only controls stay restricted |

Do not list Gemini, Termii, Google Sheets, Airtable or Vercel as universal platform
processors merely because other agency demos used them. Inventory actual optional
integrations. Do not promise Nigerian-only hosting, no international transfers or
end-to-end encryption of stored business content. Questions can contain personal
information. Verify provider contracts, locations and transfer safeguards before
publishing the final processor register and processing agreement.

Automatic customer-record deletion is disabled. The 90/180/365-day settings are
previews only. Trial expiry and subscription cancellation preserve records. Encrypted
backups have a 14-daily-copy target, not a universal deletion deadline. Provider copies
need separate handling. Define actual category-specific retention periods and a tested
closure/deletion process before publishing definitive storage-period promises.

Contact support for access, correction, export, objection/restriction, erasure or
applicable consent-withdrawal requests. Verify identity proportionately without
passwords/OTPs. Customer-business involvement and lawful retention exceptions may
apply. A person can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission; contacting
support first is not a condition on applicable rights. Self-service CSV export is not
a complete account/conversation export or automated deletion tool.

Review against [NDPC guidance](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf)
and the actual data inventory. This draft does not certify legal compliance.

## 3. Getting started — customer owners and staff

1. Sign in with your business email and enter the code only on the page. Support
   never needs your OTP. Retry a failed security check manually; do not disable it.
2. Create a workspace. Save your business type, channels and optional appointment/team
   checklist choices. These choices do not publish a website or connect WhatsApp.
3. Save your business description and HTTPS contact link; open the link yourself.
   Add accurate approved FAQs and prices. Use invented information during tests.
4. Review Lagos working days/hours, appointment duration, buffer and minimum notice,
   then save. Review welcome, away and handover responders before enabling them.
5. Start the free trial in Plans if eligible. Usage shows lifetime trial balance and
   expiry; paid plans use monthly counters. No automatic paid conversion occurs.
6. Practise a question and team reply in Conversations. Rehearsals send no WhatsApp
   message. Team takeover pauses automation; read the conversation before resuming.
7. Invite colleagues only when needed. They sign in using the exact verified invited
   email and accept. An email invitation alone grants no access. Remove former staff.
8. Review Today for enquiries, upcoming appointments and paused conversations. An
   internal follow-up has an assignee, Lagos due time and note. It sends no reminder
   email/customer message; completing it does not resume conversation automation.
9. Prepare the allowed-domain website installation and test on the actual site.
   WhatsApp needs official onboarding and an eligible number. Do not delete a Business
   app account based on a generic guide; first confirm coexistence/number treatment.
10. Review Launch. A 6/6 checklist means preparation, not verified delivery or paid
    production activation. Test the selected channels before promising availability.

For records, Inbox exports enquiries/bookings by up to 31 creation-date days, maximum
500 records/2 MB. Shorten the range when needed. Store downloaded contacts privately.
Exports exclude conversations and some private/provider records. Retention remains
preview-only; contact support for closure/privacy requests.

Report issues to admin@triumphantech.com with business ID, page, Lagos time, expected
result and a redacted screenshot. For payment issues include the reference, never card
details. Do not send secrets or full customer conversations. Identify staging/test mode.
