# TriumphantTech business automation — proposed offer

Version: 28 October 2026. Owner-approved pricing direction; publication draft.
Support/refund wording, taxes and technical launch gates still require review.
Paid activation stays disabled until the remaining launch gates are accepted.

## What a customer buys

A private business workspace for approved answers, standard enquiries,
appointment rules, autoresponders, team handover and internal follow-ups.
The website assistant and one eligible WhatsApp number use the same workspace.
WhatsApp becomes available only after number eligibility, connection and delivery
checks. Website design, bespoke workflows and third-party messaging fees are
separate. This is a software subscription, not a promise of sales or a human
customer-service team answering the business's customers.

card, automatic charge or automatic paid conversion. Expiry should stop trial
automation while preserving private access to existing records. Trial credits

is NOT implemented yet and is not the existing unrestricted staging sandbox.

Managed service: from NGN 75,000/month under a separate written scope for setup,
optimisation and ongoing assistance. It is not a fourth self-service software

## How usage works

- A reply is a metered automated response through the platform, not a unique
  customer, conversation or guaranteed delivered WhatsApp message. Website and
  connected WhatsApp automation share the allowance. A handover/fallback can
  consume a reply; an accepted WhatsApp send attempt can consume one even when
  eventual delivery fails. Idempotent retries do not create an extra allowance
  charge. Human replies and internal follow-ups are not sold as automated replies.
- Counters reset at the start of each calendar month in Africa/Lagos time.
  Subscription renewals follow the provider's billing schedule, which is separate
  from the usage reset. No rollover or automatic mid-month proration is offered.
- No automatic overage charge. At a limit, the affected automated operation is
  refused; existing records remain. Do not promise unlimited intake or an
  automatic WhatsApp fallback after the answer limit is reached.
- Bookings count newly created records, including ones later cancelled. Cancelling
  a booking does not replenish that month's record allowance.
- Optional AI matching is a constrained aid for selecting approved business facts,
  not an unrestricted generative agent. The current implementation permits at
  most 100 matching attempts per workspace/month and has a shared daily safety
  cap. A 5,000-reply plan is not 5,000 AI calls. Do not market expanded AI until
  its quota, reliability and cost are separately implemented and verified.

## WhatsApp and other provider charges

The subscription includes no prepaid Meta message credits. Preferred launch
arrangement: the customer pays Meta/provider charges on their own approved
billing account. Confirm that arrangement during onboarding; do not present it
as implemented merely because it is the recommended commercial model.

Rates depend on the recipient market, message category, billing currency and
provider. Confirm the current account rate card before activating a number.
Do not promise unlimited free service replies or a fixed universal NGN message
price. Do not collect a WhatsApp wallet or rebill credits until metering,
reconciliation, limits and customer consent exist. Any intermediary subscription
or markup must be disclosed separately before agreement.

SMS/Termii, bulk marketing, voice, unrestricted image/voice AI, payroll, bank
transfers and bespoke CRM/payment integrations are outside this standard offer.
Meta approval and template decisions cannot be guaranteed by TriumphantTech.

## Setup and website services

Self-service standard setup: no mandatory setup fee. A new website is separately
quoted and does not silently include perpetual automation access.

Optional assisted onboarding: proposed NGN 25,000 one-off for one workspace,
up to 20 customer-supplied FAQs, one appointment policy, one existing-site embed
and one 45-minute handover session. Customer supplies approved content, domain
access and eligible Meta assets. Additional content, migration, custom code or
extra sites require a written quote. Check readiness before accepting this fee;
do not charge for a promised WhatsApp connection that is not eligible.

## Support

Proposed staffed hours: Monday–Friday, 10:00–16:00 Lagos time, excluding public
holidays. Channel: admin@triumphantech.com. Targets: access/payment or widespread
service-blocking issue acknowledged within one business day; routine setup
questions within two business days. These are initial-response targets, not
resolution deadlines, 24/7 support or an uptime SLA. Confirm staffing before
publishing these commitments.

Optional configuration-help minutes in the table cover screen sharing and help
changing business content/rules; they do not roll over. Verified platform defects,
security reports, billing corrections and statutory complaints are not refused
because that allowance is used. Extra requested configuration work: proposed
NGN 10,000/hour, minimum 30 minutes, only after a separate quote is accepted.
Never use a customer's sign-in code, impersonate them or promise provider approval.

## Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Monthly recurring billing requires explicit consent. No annual commitment in
this first offer. Cancellation stops future renewal; verified paid access lasts
to the recorded paid-through date. Plan changes remain support-assisted to avoid
a second recurring agreement; confirm price and effective date before any charge.

Proposed first-payment guarantee: ask within seven calendar days of the first
successful platform subscription payment for a full platform-fee refund. This
commercial guarantee does not replace applicable consumer rights. Separately
approved provider costs and completed custom work are not bundled into that
guarantee; lawful remedies for defective or undelivered work still apply.

Refund duplicate/unauthorised collections after verification, and investigate
undelivered or materially defective service with an appropriate remedy or refund.
For an ordinary later-month cancellation, no automatic unused-time refund is
promised, subject to applicable rights and any documented service failure.
Do not use a blanket 'digital services are non-refundable' clause.

Request via admin@triumphantech.com with business ID and payment reference—never
card details, password or OTP. Target a decision within five business days after
the necessary information arrives. Eligible refunds go to the original payment
route. TriumphantTech absorbs non-returned collection fees for a promised full
platform refund; do not deduct them secretly. Provider/bank completion can take
3–10 working days after initiation and is not guaranteed by our support target.

Refunds and their entitlement effects are operator-assisted today. Verify the
provider transaction, stop renewal as appropriate, record the decision and
reconcile access. Do not claim an automated refund feature or change paid access
without a supported, reviewed process. Prepare the refund reserve before sales.
