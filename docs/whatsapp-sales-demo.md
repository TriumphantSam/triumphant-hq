## Demonstration acceptance criteria

1. A prospect can enter a clearly branded demo from the website and receive a real WhatsApp reply on the correct number.
2. The conversation shows a practical customer journey: service discovery, qualification, useful answers, and a clear next action. Menus should guide the user rather than require them to guess keywords.
3. Lead capture produces a real, private Sheet record with a reference and an owner alert. Show evidence of storage and delivery without publishing contact details.
4. Booking opens available appointments and distinguishes a link click from a confirmed booking. Order links distinguish checkout from verified payment. Claim completion only after the provider confirms it.
5. HUMAN routes the conversation to the owner and pauses bot replies. STOP/RESUME works consistently. Marketing follow-ups require explicit opt-in and approved Meta templates.
6. The customer-facing demonstration explains which outcomes are live. Any industry examples or simulations are clearly labelled; fabricated sales, bookings or receipts are not presented as real results.
7. Business-specific names, service information, pricing, booking links, alert recipients and onboarding can be configured for future clients. Keep client data and credentials isolated.
