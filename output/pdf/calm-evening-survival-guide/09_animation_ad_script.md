# Animation Ad Script
## The Calm Evening System  ·  ₦1,500

Shoot this as kinetic motion graphics. Not a talking-head lecture.
Most people watch muted. Big on-screen words. Hard cuts. Sound hits on every cut.

Primary length: **15 seconds.** Do not go longer for the first creative.
Backup: 8-second bumper + 25-second version below.

Never say PDF. Never say planner.
Say: Calm Evening System.

---

## THE ONE TO MAKE FIRST  ·  15 SECONDS

**File name:** `CES_War_15s`
**Ratio:** 9:16 (Reels / Stories). Also export 1:1.
**Caption burned in:** YES. Assume mute.
**Voice:** Female, tired-then-firm. Nigerian English. Close to the mic.
**Music:** Low pulse under. Hard whoosh on every cut. Silence on the last line.

### 0.0 – 2.0  HOOK
**VISUAL:** Front door SLAMS open. School bag hits the floor. A tablet lights up like an alarm. The word **WAR** stamps the screen in huge type.
**VO:** The fight starts before you drop your bag.
**ON SCREEN:** THE FIGHT STARTS / BEFORE YOU DROP YOUR BAG

### 2.0 – 5.5  AGITATE
**VISUAL:** Fast cuts. Child hand grabbing a phone. “Just 10 minutes” in a chat bubble. Homework book slammed shut. Aunty nodding yes at a TV. Parent’s face in a doorway, already finished.
**VO:** Just ten minutes. I don’t have homework. Aunty let us yesterday.
**ON SCREEN:** JUST 10 MINUTES. / NO HOMEWORK. / AUNTY SAID YES.

### 5.5 – 8.5  GUILT TURN
**VISUAL:** House goes still. Empty parlour. Then a car dashboard at night. Parent’s hands on the wheel. Soft text: MAYBE I AM NOT ENOUGH — then a red X slashes it.
**VO:** Then you shout. Then the guilt. You are not a failed parent.
**ON SCREEN:** YOU ARE NOT A FAILED PARENT.

### 8.5 – 12.0  MECHANISM
**VISUAL:** Chaos timeline (4pm–9pm) snaps into five labelled rails: LAND · HOMEWORK · ONE JOB · SCREENS · COLLECT. Tablet flies into a kitchen basket. A one-page deal slams onto a fridge.
**VO:** Your evening has no rails. This is the Calm Evening System. Fifteen minutes. One deal.
**ON SCREEN:** LAND → HOMEWORK → ONE JOB → SCREENS → COLLECT

### 12.0 – 15.0  OFFER
**VISUAL:** Price punch: **₦1,500**. Then the outcome line. CTA button animates: GET IT NOW. Child bringing a tablet to the kitchen. Parent’s shoulders drop.
**VO:** One thousand five hundred naira. Calm evenings. Cooperative children. Get it tonight.
**ON SCREEN:** ₦1,500 / CALM EVENINGS. COOPERATIVE CHILDREN. / GET IT TONIGHT

**End card (last 1 second, hold):**
Calm Evening System
₦1,500 · Instant access
[Selar link / logo]

## 25-SECOND VERSION  (only if 15s wins and you want more story)

Use the same first 12 seconds.
Then add:

**12–18:** Split screen. LEFT: chasing a tablet down a corridor. RIGHT: child walking it to the kitchen. Super: YOU STOP BEING THE ENEMY.
**18–22:** The line on screen and VO: I love you. The tablet is not how I prove it. Love is not the same thing as yes.
**22–25:** ₦1,500. Instant access. Get it tonight.

Do not make a 45-second version for prospecting. Attention dies.

---

## ANIMATOR NOTES (keep it viciously simple)

- No slow pans. Cut every 1–1.5 seconds until the end card.
- Face is optional. The door, the tablet, the basket, and the type do the selling.
- Colour: dark navy house → terracotta stamps → teal rails at the turn.
- Burn captions in white with a dark bar. Huge. 5–7 words max per frame.
- The ₦1,500 must be readable at thumbnail size.
- End on the kitchen basket, not on a book cover.

| Time | Hit |
|------|-----|

| 2.0 | Notification ping |
| 5.5 | Room tone drop (almost silence) |
| 8.5 | Snap / whoosh as rails lock |
| 12.0 | Cash-soft tick + voice on the price |
| 15.0 | Hold. No extra music swell |

## WHAT NOT TO PUT IN THE ANIMATION

- Do not show a 28-page book flipping.
- Do not say “digital product” or “PDF”.
- Do not list 16 chapters.
- Do not smile-through-it lifestyle footage.
- Do not bury ₦1,500 in small type.
