Ibadan founders often ask whether to hire a freelancer or a full technology agency. Both can work. The wrong choice usually comes from mismatched scope—not from “agency vs freelance” as a moral debate.

## When a freelancer is enough

- A single landing page with clear copy already written  
- A contained bug fix or design polish  
- A short-term specialist task with a sharp brief  

## When an agency (or partner team) is safer

- Website + SEO + ongoing iteration  
- Custom application work with discovery and handover  
- Multiple stakeholders and compliance needs  
- You need continuity if one person is unavailable
