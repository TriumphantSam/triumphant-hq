This guide breaks down the **cost factors** that matter, so you can budget with clarity and avoid paying twice.

## What “website design in Ibadan” usually includes

A professional engagement is more than a homepage mockup. Typical scope covers:

1. **Structure** — what pages you need and how visitors move toward contact or purchase  
2. **Design** — brand-fit layouts that feel credible on mobile and desktop  
3. **Build** — performance, forms, integrations and content entry  
4. **Launch** — DNS, analytics, basic SEO metadata, handover  
5. **Optional care** — updates, security monitoring, iterative improvements

## The real cost drivers

### 1. Number of unique page templates
A five-page brochure site is not the same as a service business with location pages, case studies, booking flows and a blog. Every unique layout adds design and engineering time.

### 2. Content readiness
If copy, photos and service descriptions are unclear, the project slows down—or launches with weak messaging. Content clarity is often the hidden budget line.

### 3. Integrations
WhatsApp CTAs are simple. Payment gateways, booking calendars, CRM handoffs and membership areas are not. Integrations should be listed explicitly in any proposal.

### 4. SEO foundations
Technical crawlability, metadata, internal links and local relevance (Ibadan / Oyo keywords where natural) should be planned early—not bolted on after launch. If search visibility matters, start with our [SEO snapshot](/seo-snapshot) or [SEO in Ibadan](/services/seo/ibadan).

### 5. Ongoing care
Websites decay. Plugins change, content goes stale, Core Web Vitals slip. Budget for care if the site is a growth asset, not a one-off brochure.

## How to compare quotes without getting trapped

Ask every vendor:

- What is included vs optional?  
- Who writes content?  
- What does “SEO included” actually mean?  
- What happens after launch?  
- Can I see live work, not only logos?
