“Best tech company in Ibadan” is a popular search—but it is not a useful buying process by itself. Rankings and listicles cannot tell you whether a partner will finish your project, explain trade-offs, or stay reachable when something breaks.

Use this checklist instead.

## 1. Verify proof you can click

Ask for live websites, apps or SEO outcomes—not only slide decks. Read [our selected work](/work) as an example of the level of clarity you should expect from any serious partner.

## 2. Demand a real delivery process

A credible Ibadan technology partner can explain:

- Discovery and scoping  
- Milestones and review points  
- What you must provide (content, access, decisions)  
- Handover and support options  

If the process is “pay and we start coding,” walk away.

## 3. Separate agency work from local identity help

NIN enrolment and BVN support are valuable—but they are not the same as building your product website. Triumphant HQ keeps a dedicated [Local Support](/local-support) desk for identity services so agency projects stay focused.

## 4. Check local responsiveness

Ibadan businesses often need WhatsApp-speed coordination and someone who understands Southwestern Nigeria markets. Remote-only vendors can work—but confirm timezone overlap and communication norms up front.

## 5. Watch for red flags

- Guaranteed #1 rankings  
- Prices with no scope  
- No live references  
- Pressure to pay 100% upfront with no milestones  
- “SEO included” with no definition  

## 6. Compare engagement models

Not every problem needs a full agency. Sometimes a specialist freelancer is enough; sometimes you need a partner team. See [agency vs freelancer](/compare/agency-vs-freelancer) and our deeper [Ibadan technology partner guide](/ibadan-tech-agency).
