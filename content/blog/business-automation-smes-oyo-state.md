SMEs across Ibadan and wider **Oyo State** lose hours every week to manual follow-up: unanswered leads, spreadsheet chaos, and staff chasing the same updates.

Automation should start with the boring bottlenecks—not a flashy app rebuild.

## High-ROI starting points

1. **Lead response** — auto-acknowledge and route WhatsApp or form enquiries  
2. **Reminders** — appointments, payment nudges, renewal dates  
3. **Handoffs** — when sales closes, operations gets a clean checklist  
4. **Reporting** — simple weekly summaries instead of manual compilation  

Use our [automation checklist](/automation-checklist) to see where you stand.

## When to choose custom applications instead

If your process is unique, compliance-heavy or too tangled for off-the-shelf tools, explore [custom applications](/services/app-development). Many Oyo State teams start with automation, then graduate to software once the workflow is clear.
