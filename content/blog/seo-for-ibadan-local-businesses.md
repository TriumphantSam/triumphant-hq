If your customers are in Ibadan, ranking for generic global keywords is not the first job. You need **local relevance**: clear pages, a consistent business profile, and technical foundations Google can trust.

## 1. Fix technical health first

Slow pages, broken mobile layouts and crawl errors waste every other effort. A short diagnostic—like our [SEO snapshot](/seo-snapshot)—shows what to fix before you write more content.

## 2. Align NAP everywhere

Name, Address and Phone must match across your website, Google Business Profile and directories. Our canonical address is Basorun Rd, Ibadan 211107, Oyo.

## 3. Build pages for how people search

Useful combinations for Ibadan firms often include:

- Service pages (e.g. website design, clinic services)  
- Location relevance ([Ibadan](/locations/ibadan), neighbourhoods when truly relevant)  
- Proof pages (work, FAQs, process)  

Avoid thin doorway pages that repeat the same paragraph with a different area name.

## 4. Treat Google Business Profile as a ranking asset

Categories, photos, posts, Q&A and reviews influence local pack visibility. Website SEO and GBP reinforce each other.

## 5. Write for humans and answer engines

Clear definitions, FAQs and consistent facts help classic search and AI-generated answers. See our [Ibadan technology partner](/ibadan-tech-agency) page for an example of useful, non-spammy authority content.
